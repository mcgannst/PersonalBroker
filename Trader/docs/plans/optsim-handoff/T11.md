- Prompts are created by `host.sync_prompts()` from `prompts(ctx)`. An answer is applied at once if `market.is_open`, otherwise by the next `opt_daily`.
- State scopes in `option_strategy_state`: `account` (`new_positions_paused`) and `pos:<wheel position id>`.
- Alert dedupe keys: `wheel:<KIND>:<pos id>:<date>` and `wheel:BENCHMARK_REVIEW_DUE:<date>`. Order reasons: `wheel: open put`, `wheel: <ACTION>`, `wheel: owner chose to close` / `... to sell`.
- `watch_underlyings` returns candidate and approved tickers, open positions and the benchmark.

## Behaviour chosen (owner-visible)
- The plug-in cannot refresh facts: a Saturday candidate with no facts row is added but screened (and its candidate prompt raised) only after the next 08:15 refresh and `opt_daily`.
- `EARLY_ASSIGNMENT_RISK` raises no prompt: a journal row plus the `EX_DIVIDEND_AHEAD` alert; default is accept.
- The wheel skips its own `STRIKE_TOUCHED` alert when `options.strike_touch_alerts` is on (the worker sends it).
- Roll: `net_limit = options.tick_size`, `walk=False`: fills at the market's net, never for a debit.
- The owner's "roll" on the earnings review uses the §8.1 pick without its earnings condition, and still respects `max_rolls_per_put`.
- A fresh-cash answer is cleared whenever a call closes or expires. A fresh-cash yes given when no call can be sold is not re-asked until `stops_fresh_cash_retest_days` have passed.
- Shares sold (not only called away) also return the ticker to `candidate`.
- Caution acknowledgements cover one entry (cleared when the put is sold). A skipped caution (`k`) is not re-asked in that cycle; the panel's `ack_caution` unblocks it.
- Entries go in rank order and stop at the first ticker whose collateral no longer fits.
- `price` in the facts is replaced by the live underlying quote at evaluation time.
- Dividends are not tracked (`wheel_positions.dividends` stays 0). The quarter-end review has no journal row (a note, an alert and the `account` state).
- Panel changes are stamped `updated_by = "owner:panel"` (the context carries no actor; the host's event_log row has the real one).
- The "evaluate" journal row is written before the host submits: a crash in between loses that day's action for that position (re-evaluated next session).
