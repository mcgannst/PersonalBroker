## Behaviour
- `official_close(market, calendar, clock, underlying, day)`: for today once the session has closed, the share quote's `last_regular` from `market.underlying_quote`, else today's daily candle; for any earlier day only that day's candle from `market.daily_bars(ticker, day, day)`. A source that raises counts as "no close yet".
- A missing close raises `PostcloseIncomplete` (a plain `RuntimeError`) after every other step ran; the runner retries it. Without a `retry` policy it fails once and waits for a manual run.
- Undelivered lifecycle events are also read back from `opt_lifecycle_events` (`delivered_at` NULL) and messaged and delivered, so a crash between settling and delivering does not leave the plug-in untold.
- The post-close job skips before the session close (a manual mid-session run would otherwise expire live day orders).
- The snapshot is written BEFORE the plug-ins' `postclose` event, so `ctx.equity_on(today)` answers. The snapshot row is keyed `(run_id, session close time)` and rewritten on a re-run; `settled_cash` equals `cash`; the peak comes from earlier snapshots only.
- A plug-in whose `postclose` event fails also fails the job (retried). `host.fire` is always called with `force=False`; to re-run one: `options-event <strategy> postclose --force`.
- **The summary is sent on the first attempt even when that attempt fails, and never again** (dedupe key `opt:summary:<date>`). A retry that later settles the expiry does not send a corrected summary.
- "Realized today" in the summary is the realized P&L of structures closed that ET day, by source; partial closes are not counted.
- No separate benchmark or quarter-end step: the benchmark ticker is in the refresh set and the quarter-end review is the plug-in's own `postclose` handler.
- The refresh calls `market.refresh_chain(ticker, force=True)` (one chain call per ticker each morning, plus about 2 s per ticker in `FactsService.refresh`); it fails (`RefreshFailed`) only when every ticker failed; partial failures are one `warning` event, source `options.refresh`.
- The adjustment check runs in both jobs. The post-close job calls `host.ensure_defaults()` before firing events.
