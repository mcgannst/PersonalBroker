## Import and construction
- `from trader.options.collateral import DefaultCollateralEngine`; `DefaultCollateralEngine()` takes no arguments; stateless, sync, pure. `evaluate(req: OrderRequest, book: CollateralBook) -> CollateralDecision`.
- State it reads from `CollateralBook`: `account.cash` and `account.equity` only; `structures` (EVERY open structure of the run, all sources and underlyings, with positions, `reserved_cash`, `cover_structure_id`, `frozen`, `source`); `working_orders` (all working orders with legs, `qty`, `intent`, `structure_id`, `reserved_cash`, `source`); `contracts`; `quotes` by contract id; `share_quotes` by ticker; `settings`; `today` (a contract is expired when `expiry < today`).

## Contract with the broker (T5): verify end to end in T16
- **`reserve_cash` is the TOTAL reserve of the structure after the fill**, not an increment: for an open the new structure's reserve, for a close or roll the structure's new reserve (0 when nothing short remains). On fill the broker calls `set_reserved(structure, decision.reserve_cash)` as is.
- While an order is working, hold `max(0, decision.reserve_cash - structure's current reserve)` plus debit and fees; holding the full `reserve_cash` double counts.
- **`OrderRequest` carries no order id**, so at the fill-time re-check the broker must drop that order from `CollateralBook.working_orders` (T5 says it does). Otherwise: false `insufficient_cash`, false `nothing_to_close`, or false `naked_short` for a covered call.
- Store `decision.cover_structure_id` on a new covered-call structure (the engine uses it to know which shares a call relies on). A buy-write returns None.
- A structure the request names must be in `book.structures`, or the result is `invalid_order`.

## Rules worth knowing
- Free cash = `account.cash` minus the `reserved_cash` of every structure and every working order in the book (`account.reserved` and `account.free_cash` are ignored).
- Spread kind is decided by strikes (long strike protective = debit), not by the sign of the net.
- Share cover must come from ONE structure with enough free shares (the order's own, else another of the same source); two 100-share structures do not jointly cover one 2-contract call leg (`naked_short`).
- A partial close that leaves a short put is accepted as cash-secured (reserve rises to strike x 100, then the cash check applies); only a short call left without a long leg or shares gives `not_covered_after_close`.
- Reserve is summed per short-expiry group, so a long leg never offsets a short that outlives it.
- **Cap:** an order that does not raise the underlying's exposure is never capped (a covered call against assigned shares passes even when the shares alone exceed the cap); closing orders never fail the cap.
- `strategies_paused` rejects every non-manual order, closes included.
- Limit orders with a `net_limit` need no quotes; `no_quote` applies only to market orders and walk orders without a limit.
